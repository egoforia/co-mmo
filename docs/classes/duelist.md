# Duelist

*"The Mirage Palace is real enough, traveller, whatever the dunes tell you."* (Sultana Zahra)

The Duelist is fast, close and deadly. They dash in, land hit after hit to
build Momentum, then spend it on finishers. They are hard to pin down and
hit harder than anyone up close, but they cannot take a beating the way a
Warden can. Overview of all Callings: [classes.md](../classes.md).

## Story

The Duelists were the Sun-court blades who guarded the desert's rulers.
When the Sun King wished to rule forever, his Duelists refused to follow
him into the tomb. They left to serve the rulers who came after, and the
Mirage Palace has kept their dancing-floor ever since. Sultana Zahra still
trains a few. She says a Duelist's real weapon is timing, and that the Sun
King had none.

## How to play

- **Alone:** Lunge in, keep hitting to build Momentum, Whirl or Execute at
  5 pips. Riposte the big hit you see coming, and Sidestep out when hurt.
  Sandstorm is the safer Path alone; Thirsting Blade keeps you fighting.
- **In a party:** let the Warden take the first hits, then go in. Your
  damage wins fights, but threat is real: Mirage gets creatures off you.
- **Weapons:** the sword is even; twin blades love Agility and crits;
  the greatsword loves Might, hits two creatures and staggers.
- **Attributes:** Might for big finishers, Agility for crits and speed, a
  little Vigor if you keep falling.
