# Ranger

*"Keep your eyes to the wind and your bow strung."* (Zephyr Falcon)

The Ranger fights from afar. Standing still and unhurt, they gather Focus,
and a shot loosed at full Focus hits like a falling star. Traps hold
creatures back, and Wildkin Rangers fight beside a spirit hawk. Overview of
all Callings: [classes.md](../classes.md).

## Story

The Rangers were the wind-watchers who kept the high paths between the
peoples. From the towers of what are now the Sky Ruins they watched every
road and warned of every danger. When the Hush came, there was nothing to
see: no army, no monster, only a slow forgetting. The towers emptied one by
one. The Rangers believe the Hush began where nobody was looking, and that
watching again is how it ends.

| Mentors | Zephyr Falcon (Sanctum), the Windkeeper (Sky Ruins) |

Until a region of the Sky Ruins is in the world, Zephyr Falcon runs the
trial in the Sanctum and the Windkeeper waits to be wished into being.

## How to play

- **Alone:** set a Snare Trap, step back, build Focus while creatures walk
  in, Aimed Shot at full Focus, Tumble when they reach you. Wildkin is the
  easier Path alone: the hawk takes some hits.
- **In a party:** you are the one who can stand still, so stand where the
  Warden keeps creatures busy and keep Focus high. Hawk Mark the creature
  everyone should hit first.
- **Weapons:** the bow has the longest reach; the sling leaves your off hand
  free and fires fast; javelins hit hard and slow.
- **Attributes:** Agility first; Wit gives Energy and shorter cooldowns.
