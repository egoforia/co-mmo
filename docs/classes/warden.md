# Warden

*"Mossgate fell long ago, but someone must still keep the gate."* (Sir Alder)

The Warden stands in front. They wear heavy armour, carry a shield, and make
creatures fight them instead of their friends. Every blow they take makes
them steadier. Overview of all Callings: [classes.md](../classes.md).

## Story

The Wardens were the knights of Mossgate, sworn to hold its gate for
whoever needed shelter. When Mossgate wished for an army that would never
tire, the Wardens were the ones who stood in the way of what it became.
They lost. Sir Alder is the last of them, still keeping a gate with nothing
left behind it. The young God's Wishbearers are the first new Wardens in an
age, and Sir Alder would like very much to see the order live again.

## How to play

- **Alone:** pull two or three creatures, Challenge, and let Resolve fill;
  Bulwark at full Resolve absorbs a lot. You kill slowly but almost never
  fall. Oakheart is the safer Path alone.
- **In a party:** start every fight with Challenge or Shield Bash so
  creatures look at you before the Duelist and Wishweaver hit them. Hold the
  Line when the healer is struggling.
- **Attributes:** free points into Vigor for safety, into Might to kill
  faster (Stonefist), or a little Spirit for self-healing.
