# Bellkeeper

*"Every bell in this temple rings for a wish someone made. Listen, and you can hear the world growing."* (Brother Tolle)

The Bellkeeper heals with bells. Each heal leaves an **Echo** on the ally,
and tolling the great bell makes every Echo ring again. They raise fallen
friends, wash away poisons and curses, and Dirgebells toll doom on
creatures. Overview of all Callings: [classes.md](../classes.md).

## Story

The Bellkeepers are the order of the Temple of the Hundred Bells, whose
bells ring for every wish. In
the Listener's time they rang constantly; in the Hush the bells went quiet,
and most of the order drifted away. Brother Tolle stayed, polishing silent
bells. The morning the young God woke, every bell in the temple rang at
once. The Bellkeepers believe a ringing bell keeps the Hush away from
anyone who hears it, and they ring for the living, for the fallen, and for
creatures that need to be laid to rest.

who learned it before Callings, it stays a common technique at its old

## How to play

- **Alone:** you kill slowly but hardly ever fall. Fight with the
  bell-mace (it heals you when no one else is hurt), Chime when surrounded,
  and Mend yourself. Dirgebell's Knell turns every hit into healing.
- **In a party:** keep Echoes on the Warden and whoever is in melee, and Toll
  when several are hurt at once. Peal of Waking clears poisons and raises a
  friend before they have to walk back from the shrine.
- **Weapons:** the bell-mace is for the front with a shield; the censer
  hits groups; the hand bell lets you stay at range with a lantern.
- **Attributes:** Spirit for bigger heals and Energy; Vigor to survive at
  the front; Wit for more Energy in long fights.
