# The Western Mountain of Frostpeak: Mount Zephyr

This document records the lore, history, geography, architecture, people, and quests of **Mount Zephyr**, the towering mountain dominating the western reaches of **Frostpeak**, the ancestral home of the Falcon beastfolk.

---

## 1. Overview & Geography

To the north of the Sanctum lies **Frostpeak**: a realm of frozen pines, silent glacial lakes, and wind-scoured drifts. While the eastern glades remain the domain of solitary snow hares and crag rams—where the elder Ysolde tends her watch-fire—the **entire western expanse is crowned by Mount Zephyr** (*Zephyr's Crag*).

Mount Zephyr is an immense spire of living white-and-cream marble layered over ancient granite. Its sheer cliffs are perpetually lashed by freezing zephyrs, but its stone holds a subtle geothermal warmth deep within. Sculpted directly into its terraced slopes is the vertical Falcon settlement of **Whiteledge**, anchored by the soaring white citadel known as **The Eyrie of the First Watch** (*The Marble Eyrie*).

## 2. Histories & Myths

### Myth I: The First Carving of Mount Zephyr
In the dawn age before towns or borders, the northern gales tore flesh and feather alike from any birdfolk attempting to roost on the high cliffs. The earliest Falcons gathered at the peak during a terrifying winter and made their first wish to the **Listener**:
> *"Grant us eyes to see every road, and stone that yields like down to our talons."*

The Listener heard their prayer. Where the first Falcon claws struck, the forbidding dark granite softened into gleaming cream and white marble, as pliant as chalk to their silver chisels yet impervious to the fiercest winter frost. 

The Falcon people never felled trees for timber nor mixed mortar to bind stone. Instead, they sculpted their halls, plazas, balustrades, and homes out of the living mountain itself.

### Myth II: The Hoarfrost Roc and the Great Forgetting
Before the **Hush**, the **Hoarfrost Roc** did not nest alone on the desolate northern crags. It was the bonded celestial guardian of Mount Zephyr, roosting atop **Floor +8: The Crown of the Wind**. When winter blizzards threatened to bury the terraced homes, the Roc spread its colossal wings across the open balconies, shielding the hearths below.

When the Listener fell silent and the Hush drifted across the land, the Roc slowly forgot its sacred oath. Its memories crystallized into ice, its bond with the Falcons dissolved, and it drifted north to brood over a solitary nest—guarding only its forgotten past.

### Myth III: The Sunken Drafts and the Lost High Way
Deep beneath Mount Zephyr run four subterranean storeys known as **The Sunken Drafts**. In ancient times, geothermal vents warmed these cavern roads, creating a subterranean highway where Falcon scouts could travel between the northern mountains and the southern Sanctum unhindered by surface blizzards. 

When the Hush came, parts of the lower tunnels collapsed, and the deepest chambers (-2 to -4) were claimed by frost-forged horrors and restless Ice Wisps welling up from the subterranean glacier.

## 3. The Settlement: Whiteledge (Floor 0)

Whiteledge is built on stepped marble terraces cut into the western cliffs. It is shielded from the cruelest polar winds by the crest of the mountain.

- **The Grand Hearth:** A sunken circular courtyard with a great bronze brazier burning sweet, resinous frost-pine logs day and night.
- **The Sculpted Houses:** Dwellings hollowed out of the marble face, featuring leaded frosted-glass dormers and smooth fluted columns.
- **Winding Stairs:** Broad marble steps linking the lower trail from Ysolde's clearing to the citadel gates.

## 4. Vertical Architecture: The Eyrie of the First Watch

The central monument of Mount Zephyr rises 8 floors into the clouds and descends 4 floors into the earth.

### Above Ground (Floors +1 to +8)

- **Floor +1: The Grand Portico & High Archway**
  The marble gates and entry colonnade connecting Whiteledge town to the interior of the spire.
- **Floor +2: The Loom-Terraces**
  Sunlit weaving ateliers where Sula spins Mountain Wool and Snow Hare Fur into winter mantles and reinforced leather quivers.
- **Floor +3: The Wind-Scriptorium**
  Chambers lined with marble bookshelves housing ancient migration maps, wind treatises, and histories of the Listener's silence.
- **Floor +4: The Steaming Marble Baths**
  An open-air terrace with naturally heated mineral pools fed by geothermal steam conduits rising from Floor -1.
- **Floor +5: The Master Falconer's Roost & Archery Balcony**
  Cantilevered outdoor platforms where Rangers train their focus and shoot at targets hovering across deep mountain gorges.
- **Floor +6: High Council Chamber & Feather Archive**
  Circular meeting hall adorned with white silk banners where Elder Aurius and the council convene.
- **Floor +7: The Belfry of Zephyrs**
  An open belfry housing silver wind-harps and tuned bells that chime when blizzards or hostile creatures approach.
- **Floor +8: The Crown of the Wind**
  The highest open peak of Mount Zephyr. Features an ancient astrolabe, a panoramic view of Wishmere, the empty roosting dais of the Hoarfrost Roc, and Watcher Vail keeping his eternal vigil.

### Below Ground: The Sunken Drafts (Floors -1 to -4)

- **Floor -1: Upper Silver Veins & Snowbell Caverns**
  A transition realm between civilization and the deep wilds. Warm moisture creates an environment where glowing Snowbells bloom along silver ore seams. Kip the Spelunker maintains an expedition camp near the mine elevator.
- **Floor -2: The Whispering Tunnels**
  A vast, labyrinthine network of draft-hollowed caves. Swarms of aggressive **Ice Wisps** and cunning **Rime Stalkers** stalk the icy corridors.
- **Floor -3: Crypt of the First Watchers**
  A solemn underground necropolis of white marble tombs, guarded by ancient warding runes and territorial **Deep Crag Matriarchs**.
- **Floor -4: The Hoarfrost Well & Frozen Vault**
  A glacial chasm at the base of the mountain where ancient wishstuff has frozen into indestructible blue ice. High-level frost monsters guard sealed relics from the First Wish.

## 5. Notable Inhabitants (NPCs)

### 1. Talon-Mason Kaen (Whiteledge Plaza, Floor 0)
- **Role:** Master Carver of the Mountain.
- **Personality:** Proud, patient, and philosophical. Speaks of stone as a living, breathing partner.
- **Shop:** Sells stone chisels, carving kits, and marble building blocks.
- **Voice:** *"Lowlanders stack brick upon brick and pray the mortar holds. We carve away only what the mountain does not need, and what remains lasts forever."*

### 2. Sula the Feather-Weaver (Floor +2)
- **Role:** Master Tailor & Leatherworker.
- **Personality:** Warm, sharp, meticulous with needle and loom.
- **Shop:** Advanced leatherworking tools, Mountain Wool cloaks, quiver upgrades.
- **Voice:** *"The wind here will freeze the blood in your veins unless you wear what the mountain provided. Bring me ram's wool and hare's down, and I will weave you a second skin."*

### 3. Elder Aurius the Keen (Floor +6)
- **Role:** High Elder of Mount Zephyr.
- **Personality:** Grave and dignified; carries a long silver-tipped staff. Remembers when the Sky Ruins and Frostpeak were united under one watch.
- **Voice:** *"Every gust of wind tells a story, Wishbearer. Lately, they whisper of bells ringing in the southern Sanctum. Has the young God truly awakened?"*

### 4. Watcher Vail (Floor +8, The Crown of the Wind)
- **Role:** The Faithful Watcher of the Roc.
- **Personality:** Solitary, quiet, unwavering in devotion.
- **Voice:** *"I light the silver incense every morning so the Roc might smell her old roost upon the wind. She has forgotten us, but we have not forgotten her."*

### 5. Kip the Spelunker (Whiteledge / Floor -1 Descent)
- **Role:** Scout & Delver of the Sunken Drafts.
- **Personality:** Energetic, daring, perhaps slightly reckless.
- **Voice:** *"Everyone else looks up at the sky, but all the real treasures are down where the mountain breathes cold through its teeth! Care to take the plunge?"*

## 7. Extended Quest Chain: The Awakening of Zephyr

1. **"Lights on the Cliffs"** *(Elder Ysolde, Frostpeak Glade)*
   - Defeat 5 Ice Wisps roaming the surface.
   - Upon completion, Ysolde remarks: *"These wisps aren't descending from the clouds; they are welling up from the Sunken Drafts under Mount Zephyr. Seek out Kip at the western descent."*
2. **"Into the Sunken Drafts"** *(Kip the Spelunker, Floor -1)*
   - Delve into Floor -2 and defeat 4 Ice Wisps and 2 Rime Stalkers.
   - Recover the **Guardian's Silver Harness** lost in the chasm.
3. **"A Thread to Remember"** *(Sula Feather-Weaver, Floor +2)*
   - Deliver the ancient harness, 3 Mountain Wool, and 2 Snow Hare Fur to restore the ceremonial mantle.
   - Sula crafts the **Zephyr Plume Mantle** (cold-warding gear).
4. **"The Crown Without a King"** *(Watcher Vail, Floor +8)*
   - Carry the restored offering to the highest roost on Floor +8 and ignite the brazier.
   - Vail sends you north to confront the Hoarfrost Roc, not to slay it out of malice, but to break the rime of the Hush around its heart!
